A personal note

Room to be myself

I take my time getting close to people. I value kindness, discretion, and the comfort of a friend who knows when to listen. Privacy is one way I look after the relationships that matter to me.

As a Muslim woman, I choose modest clothing as an expression of my own values. I enjoy the freedom in choosing a fabric, colour, or cut that feels right for me. There’s confidence in feeling at home in what I wear.

My abaya business

The abaya edit

I sell abayas and modest clothing for Muslim women. When we talk about a piece, I care about how it moves through your day. These are three details I like to explore.

Drape

How a fabric falls and moves as you walk.

Silhouette

An easy line, comfortable sleeves, and room to move.

Finishing

A quiet cuff or delicate trim that rewards a closer look.

Concept collection · fabric swatches are illustrative

Looking ahead

I hope to develop a thoughtful selection of abayas and become someone women feel comfortable asking about fit and coverage. I’d like my business to grow at a pace that leaves time for each conversation.

Styling notebook

The line of a sleeve

Before I would photograph an abaya for a collection, I would want to see it both still and moving. A sleeve resting at the wrist tells one story. Reaching for a book tells another.

Collection study · awaiting a garment

The first entry will pair a full-length photograph with a close view of the cuff. Fabric, cut and styling notes will be added once a specific piece is documented.

My mission

Room for personal choice

I want women to feel comfortable choosing a silhouette that fits their faith and their everyday lives. Sometimes the most expressive choice is a quiet one: the weight of a fabric or the space a sleeve gives you to move.

A styling question to explore
