Behind the bottle

I wear my mood.

I’m Sumaiya. If a room goes quiet, I usually have a question, a joke, or a very strong opinion ready. I like being seen, and I love it when an unexpected conversation gives me a new way to see someone else.

Perfume gives that expressive side of me another language. A bright opening feels like the first hello. The quieter notes that stay on my sleeve feel a little more personal.

My mission

A scent that feels like you.

I want you to have the language to choose for yourself. Tell me about a fragrance you loved, or one that felt too strong. That small detail is more helpful to me than the prettiest label on the shelf.
